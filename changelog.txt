Version: 0.3.6
Date: 2026-10-13
  Changes:
    - With fse-std 0.8: the floating slot is drawn at fse-std's "Window size" setting (the slot, the grip, the undo
      button and the drag bar grow with it), on top of the game's UI scale. Its first spot left of the character
      screen and the spot it lands on when dragged follow that size, and it stays on the screen when the size changes.
    - The slot docked on a window keeps the game's UI scale, like that window's own slots, so it still lines up with
      the inventory. Needs fse-std 0.8.0 for the window size (older fse-std: as before).

Version: 0.3.5
Date: 2026-10-12
  Changes:
    - One undo: what a delete, a right-click or trashing a new stack destroys is kept. A flying text at the cursor
      names it, and an undo button beside the slot (shown only then, its tooltip naming the stack) puts it back into
      the inventory. The next destroy replaces it. Its place stays reserved while there is nothing to undo, so the
      slot's frame doesn't change size when it comes and goes.
    - The grip shows four arrows ("move") instead of the game's ">" (a dark one on light themes).
    - The grip, the undo button and the drag bar are 24 px (were 20 and 10), easier to hit.
    - The first time the slot floats it sits left of the character screen, from the screen's size and scale (was a
      fixed 200, 200).
    - With FSE std themes: padding 6, the grip themed, its icon dark on light themes.
